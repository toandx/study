1. Different Claude model reasoning vs Thinking capability?
- Increase reasoning effort allow Claude to spend more compute on planning, analysis and solving complex problem (multi-step reasoning). Reasoning level: Low (Fast), Medium, High (Max reasoning for complex coding).
- Claude Thinking enable model to perform additional internal reasoning before generating a response, improving it's ability to solve complex problems. 
- When thinking is enabled, Claude analyzes the prompt, plans it approach, and produces a summarized thinking block before the final response.
- After complete its reasoning, Claude return a final response to user, while the thinking process remain separate from the final answer. 
- Thinking là lượng thời gian/token mà Claude được phép dùng để suy nghĩ trước khi trả lời. Question -> Answer -> Thinking, analyze -> Answer -> Thinking -> Answer
- Claude thinking is a step before reasoning really happens, where reasoning control how much compute Claude allocates to its reasoning process.
- Thinking is best for complex planning, debugging, math reasoning, and multi step problem solving.
- Reasoning is best for balance response quality, latency and cost based on task complexity.
3. What problem of LLM?
- LLM are trained on static data, LLM don't know your private or lastest data, 
- Retrain or fine-tune is slow, expensive, and brittle.
- High risk of hallucination

Why finetune falls short?
- Require new data and label
- Multi iteration: Collect data -> Retrain -> Finetune -> Evaluate => Hard to keep model up to date
- Not ideal for frequently changing knowledge
- Operationally heavy for enterprise system.

2. What is RAG? (Retrieval augmented generation)
- In Retrieval augmented generation, we task foundation-large language model. 
- Before generate that retrieval pipeline, we focus on arranging our enterprise documents in the form of a database.
corpus: Tập ngữ liệu
- Once that retrieval pipeline functions very well, we take the grounding knowledge, we take the user query and then send that as a final prompt to LLM, and boom, you have a chatbot that's built on top of your enterprise knowledge.
- In RAG pipeline, you don't train the model, but you focus on generating or establishing database of unstructured document. 
- RAG pipeline = Retrieval (Search for relevant info in Google) -> Augmented (Feed retrieved content into model) -> Generation (Produce a natural language response)
- Xây dựng LLM cơ bản, hỏi một kiến thức mới. LLM ko biết câu trả lời. RAG cho phép tìm kiếm thông tin bên ngoài, đưa vào model, trả lời. 
4. Why RAG is critical in Enterprise AI?
- Keep response up to date
- Solve hallucination, freshness and private-data limitation of LLMs
- Work with private and proprietary data
- Fastest and cheaper than fine-tune.
5. What is Vector Embedding? 
- Vector embedding are a way of representing words, sentences, images or other data as numerical vector in a high dimensional space.
- These vectors capture the semantic or contextual information in the data, allow similar items to be closer in that space.
